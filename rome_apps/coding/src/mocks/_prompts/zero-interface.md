## Visual style: Zero Interface
- **Type:** General  ·  **Origin/Era:** 2020s AI-Era  ·  **Complexity:** Low
- **Look & feel / keywords:** Minimal visible UI, voice-first, gesture-based, AI-driven, invisible controls, predictive, context-aware, ambient
- **Art direction:** Create a voice-first, gesture-based, AI-driven interface with minimal visible UI, progressive disclosure, voice recognition UI, gesture detection, AI predictions, smart suggestions, context-aware actions. Hide controls until needed.
- **Primary colors:** Neutral backgrounds: Soft white #FAFAFA, light grey #F0F0F0, warm off-white #F5F1E8
- **Secondary colors:** Subtle feedback: light green, light red, minimal UI elements, soft accents
- **Effects & animation:** Voice recognition UI, gesture detection, AI predictions (smooth reveal), progressive disclosure, smart suggestions
- **Key design values:** --voice-ui: enabled, --gesture-detection: active, --ai-predictions: smart, --progressive-disclosure: true, --visible-ui: minimal, --context-aware: true
- **Best suited for:** Voice assistants, AI platforms, future-forward UX, smart home, contextual computing, ambient experiences
- **Avoid for:** Complex workflows, data-entry heavy, traditional systems, legacy support, explicit control
- **Theme support:** Light ✓ Full · Dark ✓ Full  ·  **Mobile:** ✓ High  ·  **Conversion:** ✓ High
- **Accessibility:** ✓ Excellent  ·  **Performance:** ⚡ Excellent

## Recommended typography
- **Pairing:** Neumorphism Mobile (Plus Jakarta Sans + System) (Geometric Sans (System Fallback))
- **Heading typeface:** Plus Jakarta Sans
- **Body typeface:** Plus Jakarta Sans
- **Mood:** neumorphism, soft ui, monochromatic, cool grey, minimal, physical, depth, ceramic, system font, utility

- **Notes:** Quantum cyan + interference purple
- (The style's own Primary/Secondary colors above take precedence; use this set to fill semantic roles such as card, muted, border, ring, and destructive.)

## Visual & interaction guidelines
- Establish a clear visual hierarchy and consistent spacing, type scale, color, radius, shadow, and motion derived from the spec above.
- Provide clear interactive states: rest, hover, focus, pressed/selected, and disabled.
- Stay legible and well-composed from small mobile widths to large desktop widths; no horizontal overflow, no overlapping or clipped content.
- Maintain accessible color contrast and a clearly visible focus indicator.
- Respect a reduced-motion preference for non-essential animation.
- The interface must immediately communicate the "Zero Interface" style through color, shape, type, texture, and motion. Be distinctive and opinionated, not generic.
