Chapter 02 · The Descent

Where the light gives way to layers.

Every hundred metres reveals a new world. The team logged three distinct strata — each colder, quieter, and stranger than the last.

The Twilight Band

At 400 metres, sunlight dissolves to a single bruise-blue line. Instruments switched to thermal and the cameras began to glow.

Crystal Galleries

Pressure-formed ice arches stretched for kilometres. We mapped 14 chambers, naming each after a constellation overhead.

The Quiet Depth

Below 1,500 metres the world stilled to a single heartbeat of sonar. The longest silence any of us had ever held.

Chapter 03 · The Summit
“We came to chart the depth and found ourselves changed by the climb back up. The aurora was never beneath the ice — it was the light we carried with us.” — Dr. Naima Okafor, Expedition Lead

Notes from the ice.

Day 07 · 06:12

First anchor set

−78.4621°, 106.8377° · −41°C

Day 23 · 14:48

Crystal gallery mapped

Lidar returned a chamber the size of a cathedral. We held the lamps still for a full minute to let the sensors breathe.

Deepest reading
