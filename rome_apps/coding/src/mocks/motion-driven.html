003 — How we work

From idea to motion

A choreographed pipeline from storyboard to shippable, GPU-accelerated code.

01

Storyboard

We map the narrative beat-by-beat, defining scroll triggers, entrance moments, and the emotional arc before a single line of code.

02

Choreograph

Easing curves, layered parallax, and microinteractions are tuned on real devices with Intersection Observer driving every reveal.

03

Ship

Transform-only, will-change optimized builds with prefers-reduced-motion fallbacks baked in. It moves everywhere, or politely stops.
