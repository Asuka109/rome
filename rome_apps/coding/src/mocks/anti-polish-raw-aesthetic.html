How it gets made

  1. Pencil on the worst paper

    Thumbnails go straight into a battered A5 book. No undo, no grid.

  2. Ink, smudge, scan

    Brush pen over the lines, then flatbed-scanned at 600dpi with dust left in.

  3. Risograph or screen

    Two spot colours, deliberate mis-registration, a little ink starvation.

  4. Number & send

    Hand-stamped, signed in pencil, wrapped in kraft and posted Tuesdays.
