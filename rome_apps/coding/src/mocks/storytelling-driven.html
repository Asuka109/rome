The Origin

It started on a windowsill in a fourth-floor apartment.

Maya had no garden, no money, and no patience for grocery-store tomatoes that tasted of nothing. So she planted three seeds in a cracked mug and waited.

What grew back was not just basil. It was the stubborn idea that anyone, anywhere, could grow something real with their own hands.

The Turn

One photo. Ten thousand strangers wanted in.

Maya posted her tiny harvest online with a single caption: “You can do this too.” By morning, inboxes overflowed with people asking the same question — how do I start?

That question became our entire reason for being. Wildroot stopped being a hobby and became a promise to never let a beginner feel alone.

The Journey

Seven years, traced one root at a time.

  1. 2017

    The cracked mug

    Three basil seeds, one windowsill, and a refusal to accept flavorless food.

  2. 2019

    The first kit ships

    We mailed 300 hand-packed starter kits. All 300 plants survived their first month.

  3. 2021

    The community blooms

    Growers began teaching growers. Our forum logged its millionth message of encouragement.

  4. 2024

    A movement, rooted

    40,000 households across 11 countries now grow part of what they eat. The story is still being written.
